# Toast example

Good evening, everyone. For those who don't know me, I promised I would keep this short, and unlike the groom, I intend to keep my promises.

I have known this couple long enough to say with confidence that they are perfect for each other. One of them can't make a decision without a spreadsheet, and the other has never once read the terms and conditions. Together, they average out to one fully functional adult.

But truly: watching the two of you build a life together has been one of the great joys of my life. You make each other kinder, braver, and much better at parallel parking.

So please raise your glasses. To the happy couple — may your love be modern enough to survive the times, and old-fashioned enough to last forever. Cheers!
